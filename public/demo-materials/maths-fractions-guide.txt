SECTION 1: WHAT IS A FRACTION?

A fraction shows PART of something whole.

Think of a cake. If you cut it into 4 equal pieces and take 1 piece, you have taken ONE QUARTER (1/4) of the cake.

A fraction has two parts:
- NUMERATOR (top number) = how many parts you have
- DENOMINATOR (bottom number) = how many equal parts in total

Example: 3/4 means you have 3 parts out of 4 equal parts.

SECTION 3: FRACTIONS IN EVERYDAY LIFE

TEACHER: I want to help you see fractions around you!

AT HOME:
- Mum cuts a watermelon into 8 slices. You eat 2. You ate 2/8 of the watermelon.
- Your family of 4 shares a pizza equally. Each person gets 1/4.
- You read 5 pages of a 10-page story. You read 5/10 or 1/2 of the story.

TELLING TIME:
- Quarter past = 1/4 of an hour = 15 minutes
- Half past = 1/2 of an hour = 30 minutes
- Quarter to = 3/4 of an hour has passed = 45 minutes

MONEY:
- If you have $1 and spend 50 cents, you spent 1/2 of your money.
- If a loaf of bread costs $4 and you pay $1, you paid 1/4 of the cost.

---

SECTION 4: COMPARING FRACTIONS

QUESTION: Which is bigger, 1/2 or 1/4?

THINK about it this way:
- If you share a cake with 1 friend (2 people), each gets 1/2
- If you share with 3 friends (4 people), each gets 1/4

1/2 is BIGGER than 1/4 because you share with fewer people!

RULE: When the top number is the same, the SMALLER bottom number means a BIGGER fraction.

1/2 > 1/3 > 1/4 > 1/5

---

SECTION 5: EQUIVALENT FRACTIONS

Some fractions are EQUAL even if they look different.

Look at this:
1/2 = 2/4 = 4/8

Imagine a chocolate bar:
- Cut into 2 pieces, take 1 = 1/2
- Cut into 4 pieces, take 2 = 2/4
- Cut into 8 pieces, take 4 = 4/8

They are all the SAME amount! These are called EQUIVALENT FRACTIONS.

Good to know: To find equivalent fractions, multiply or divide both numbers by the same amount.

1/2 × 2/2 = 2/4 ✓
2/4 × 2/2 = 4/8 ✓
