TANDEMLEARN LESSON MATERIAL
Subject: Physical Education and Arts
Topic: Movement, Dance, and Creative Expression
Grade: 5

---

HELLO STUDENT!

Today we will learn about moving our bodies and expressing ourselves through art and dance. These activities are fun and help us stay healthy!

---

PART A: PHYSICAL EDUCATION

SECTION 1: WHY EXERCISE IS IMPORTANT

Moving your body every day helps you:
✓ Stay strong and healthy
✓ Have more energy for learning
✓ Sleep better at night
✓ Feel happy (exercise releases "happy chemicals"!)
✓ Make friends through games and sports

AIM: Move your body for at least 30 minutes every day!

---

SECTION 2: WARM-UP EXERCISES (Do these first!)

Before any activity, warm up your body. Do each for 30 seconds:

1. MARCHING ON THE SPOT
   - Lift your knees high
   - Swing your arms
   - Count: 1, 2, 3, 4...

2. ARM CIRCLES
   - Stretch arms out to sides
   - Make small circles, then bigger
   - Forward 10 times, backward 10 times

3. JUMPING JACKS
   - Start with feet together, arms down
   - Jump! Feet apart, arms up
   - Jump! Back to start
   - Repeat 10 times

4. STRETCHING
   - Touch your toes (keep legs straight)
   - Reach up high to the sky
   - Twist side to side gently

Now your body is ready!

---

SECTION 3: SIMPLE EXERCISE ROUTINE

Try this 10-minute workout with your family!

ACTIVITY 1: RUNNING ON THE SPOT (1 minute)
- Run fast for 20 seconds
- Walk slowly for 10 seconds
- Repeat twice

ACTIVITY 2: SQUATS (1 minute)
- Stand with feet apart
- Bend knees like sitting on a chair
- Stand up straight again
- Do 10 squats

ACTIVITY 3: STAR JUMPS (1 minute)
- Like jumping jacks but bigger!
- Jump high, spread arms and legs like a star
- Land softly
- Do 10 star jumps

ACTIVITY 4: BALANCING (1 minute)
- Stand on one foot
- Count to 10
- Change feet
- Try with eyes closed (harder!)

ACTIVITY 5: COOL DOWN (1 minute)
- Walk slowly around the room
- Take deep breaths
- Stretch gently

GOOD WORK! You exercised your whole body!

---

SECTION 4: TRADITIONAL GAMES

These games from Zimbabwe are fun to play with friends and family:

NHODO (Stone Game)
- Dig small holes in the ground
- Use small stones or seeds
- Take turns putting stones in holes
- Strategy game that makes you think!

PADA (Hopscotch)
- Draw squares on the ground
- Throw a stone into a square
- Hop on one foot, skip that square
- Good for balance and coordination

MAHUMBWE (Pretend Play)
- Act out different roles
- Pretend to be adults doing jobs
- Use imagination and creativity
- Learn about community roles

PART B: ARTS AND CREATIVITY

SECTION 5: DRAWING ACTIVITY

TODAY'S PROJECT: Draw Your Family

Materials needed:
- Paper
- Pencils, crayons, or coloured pencils

STEPS:
1. Think about your family members
2. Start with simple shapes (circles for heads, rectangles for bodies)
3. Add details: eyes, mouths, hair, clothes
4. Draw everyone doing something together (eating, playing, working)
5. Add background: your house, garden, trees

TIPS:
- Start with light pencil lines
- It's okay to make mistakes - artists always do!
- Add colours that you like
- Write names under each person

QUESTION for AI: "Give me tips on how to draw faces"

---

SECTION 6: CRAFT ACTIVITY

TODAY'S PROJECT: Paper Weaving (Basket Pattern)

This activity teaches patterns and fine motor skills!

Materials needed:
- 2 sheets of coloured paper (different colours)
- Scissors
- Glue

STEPS:
1. Take one paper - fold it in half
2. Cut lines from the fold (DON'T cut all the way!)
3. Open the paper - you have slits
4. Cut the second paper into long strips
5. Weave strips through slits: over, under, over, under
6. Glue the ends to hold

PATTERNS TO TRY:
- Basic: over 1, under 1
- Advanced: over 2, under 2
- Creative: use 3 colours!

This is like how baskets are woven in Zimbabwe!

---

SECTION 7: MUSIC AND RHYTHM

CLAPPING RHYTHMS (Do with family!)

Basic rhythm: CLAP - CLAP - REST - CLAP
Count: 1 - 2 - 3 - 4

Try this pattern:
CLAP - CLAP - CLAP - REST
STOMP - STOMP - CLAP - CLAP

BODY PERCUSSION:
- Clap hands
- Pat thighs
- Stomp feet
- Snap fingers
- Tap chest

CREATE YOUR OWN RHYTHM:
1. Choose 4 sounds
2. Put them in a pattern
3. Repeat the pattern
4. Teach it to someone!

---

SECTION 8: DANCE ACTIVITY

SIMPLE DANCE MOVES TO LEARN:

MOVE 1: THE STEP-TOUCH
- Step right foot to the side
- Bring left foot to touch
- Step left foot to the side
- Bring right foot to touch
- Repeat!

MOVE 2: THE BOX STEP
- Step forward with right foot
- Step forward with left foot
- Step back with right foot
- Step back with left foot
- You're making a box shape!

MOVE 3: THE SWAY
- Stand with feet apart
- Shift weight to right foot
- Shift weight to left foot
- Add arm movements
- Sway to music!

CHALLENGE: Put all 3 moves together in a dance!

---

SECTION 9: FAMILY ACTIVITY

DO THIS TOGETHER:

ACTIVITY: Movement Story

1. One person calls out an animal
2. Everyone moves like that animal for 30 seconds
3. Next person calls a different animal
4. Keep going!

ANIMALS TO TRY:
- Elephant (walk slowly, swing arms like trunk)
- Rabbit (hop hop hop!)
- Bird (flap arms, move lightly)
- Snake (slither on the ground)
- Lion (walk proudly, roar!)

This is fun AND exercise!
