SECTION 1: PARTS OF A PLANT

Every plant has important parts. Learn these:

ROOTS (Underground)
- Anchor the plant in soil
- Absorb water and minerals from the ground
- Store food for the plant
- Example: carrots and sweet potatoes ARE roots we eat!

STEM (Main body)
- Holds the plant up straight
- Carries water from roots to leaves
- Carries food from leaves to other parts
- Can be soft (tomato) or hard (tree trunk)

LEAVES (Green parts)
- Make food for the plant using sunlight
- This process is called PHOTOSYNTHESIS
- Take in carbon dioxide, release oxygen
- We breathe the oxygen that plants make!

FLOWERS (Colourful parts)
- Help the plant reproduce (make seeds)
- Attract bees and butterflies
- Come in many beautiful colours

FRUITS (Contain seeds)
- Protect the seeds inside
- Help spread seeds to new places
- Many fruits are food for us: mangoes, oranges, tomatoes

SEEDS (New beginnings)
- Can grow into new plants
- Contain baby plant and food store
- Some seeds we eat: beans, maize, groundnuts

SECTION 2: WHAT DO PLANTS NEED TO GROW?

Plants need 5 things to grow well. Think of them as the plant's food!

1. WATER 💧
- Plants drink through their roots
- Water carries nutrients through the plant
- Too little = plant wilts and dies
- Too much = roots can rot
- Question: How often should you water your garden?

2. SUNLIGHT ☀️
- Plants use sunlight to make their food
- Leaves catch sunlight like solar panels
- Most vegetables need 6-8 hours of sun daily
- Some plants like shade (like some ferns)

3. AIR 🌬️
- Plants breathe carbon dioxide (CO2)
- They release oxygen (O2) - what we breathe!
- Air also helps leaves stay dry and healthy

4. SOIL 🌍
- Provides minerals and nutrients
- Anchors roots in place
- Good soil holds water but also drains well
- Different plants like different types of soil

5. SPACE 📏
- Roots need room to spread
- Leaves need space to catch sunlight
- Crowded plants don't grow well
- This is why farmers plant in rows with gaps

SECTION 3: THE PLANT LIFE CYCLE

Every plant goes through stages. Learn the cycle:

STAGE 1: SEED
- The plant begins as a tiny seed
- Seeds wait for the right conditions to grow
- They need warmth and moisture to start

STAGE 2: GERMINATION (Sprouting)
- The seed coat cracks open
- A tiny root grows down into soil
- A tiny shoot grows up toward light
- The baby plant is called a SEEDLING

STAGE 3: GROWTH
- The seedling grows bigger
- More leaves appear
- Stem gets taller and stronger
- Roots spread deeper and wider

STAGE 4: FLOWERING
- The plant makes flowers
- Flowers attract pollinators (bees, butterflies)
- Pollen moves from flower to flower
- This is called POLLINATION

STAGE 5: FRUITING
- After pollination, fruits form
- Seeds develop inside the fruits
- Fruits protect the seeds

STAGE 6: SEED DISPERSAL
- Seeds spread to new places by:
  - Wind (dandelions)
  - Water (coconuts)
  - Animals (birds eating berries)
  - Humans (farmers planting)

Then the cycle begins again!

SECTION 4: FARMING IN ZIMBABWE

COMMON CROPS WE GROW:
- Maize (sadza!) - our staple food
- Groundnuts - for eating and oil
- Vegetables - tomatoes, onions, cabbages
- Fruits - mangoes, bananas, oranges
- Cotton - for making clothes
- Tobacco - cash crop

FARMING SEASONS:
- Planting season: October - December (when rains start)
- Growing season: January - March
- Harvest season: April - June

GOOD FARMING PRACTICES:
- Prepare soil before planting (dig, add manure)
- Plant at the right time
- Water regularly if rains are low
- Remove weeds that steal nutrients
- Protect plants from pests
- Harvest at the right time

SECTION 5: SIMPLE EXPERIMENT YOU CAN DO

TRY THIS AT HOME with your family:

GROW A BEAN IN A JAR

You need:
- Glass jar or clear plastic container
- Cotton wool or tissue paper
- Dried beans (sugar beans work well!)
- Water

Steps:
1. Put damp cotton wool in the jar
2. Place 2-3 beans against the glass
3. Keep cotton wool moist (not too wet)
4. Put jar in a warm, light place
5. Watch and wait!

OBSERVE:
- Day 1-2: Bean swells with water
- Day 3-4: Root appears (grows down)
- Day 5-7: Shoot appears (grows up)
- Week 2: First leaves open

QUESTION for AI: Why does the root always grow down and the shoot always grow up?
