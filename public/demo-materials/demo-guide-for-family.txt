==========================================
TANDEMLEARN DEMO GUIDE
For Amanda and Family
==========================================

Hello! This guide will help you use TandemLearn 
to experience how Amanda can learn with AI assistance.

==========================================
GETTING STARTED
==========================================

STEP 1: LOG IN
--------------
1. Go to the TandemLearn website
2. Click "Sign In" 
3. Enter your username and password
4. Click "Login"

You will see Amanda's Student Dashboard.

STEP 2: FIND YOUR LESSONS
-------------------------
On the dashboard, you will see:
- Today's lessons
- All enrolled lessons

Click on a lesson to open it.

==========================================
USING LESSON MATERIALS
==========================================

Each lesson has materials uploaded by the teacher.
Here's what you can do:

VIEWING MATERIALS
-----------------
1. Click on a lesson
2. Look for "Lesson Materials" section
3. Click on any material to see options

FOR TEXT FILES (like transcripts):
- Click "Download" to save to your device
- Click "Chat with AI" to ask questions

FOR LINKS (like YouTube videos):
- Click to open in new tab
- Watch the video together as a family

==========================================
THE AI CHAT FEATURE - VERY IMPORTANT!
==========================================

This is the BEST feature for learning!

HOW TO USE IT:
1. Open any lesson material
2. Click the "Ask AI" button (looks like a robot/chat icon)
3. A chat window opens

WHAT TO DO:
- Type your question in the box
- Press "Send" or the arrow button
- Wait for the AI to respond
- Ask follow-up questions!

EXAMPLE QUESTIONS FOR EACH SUBJECT:
-----------------------------------

ENGLISH (Nouns & Pronouns):
- "What is the difference between a noun and pronoun?"
- "Give me 5 examples of proper nouns"
- "Check if this sentence is correct: Her went to school"
- "Make a simple quiz about pronouns for me"

MATHEMATICS (Fractions):
- "Explain what 3/4 means"
- "Which is bigger: 1/2 or 1/3?"
- "Give me a word problem about fractions"
- "How do I add 1/4 + 1/4?"

AGRICULTURE (Plants):
- "What does photosynthesis mean?"
- "Why do plants need sunlight?"
- "What crops grow well in Zimbabwe?"
- "Explain the plant life cycle simply"

PE & ARTS:
- "What exercises can I do in a small room?"
- "How do I draw a face step by step?"
- "Teach me a clapping rhythm pattern"
- "What are traditional Zimbabwean games?"

==========================================
SPECIAL FEATURES FOR AMANDA
==========================================

TEXT-TO-SPEECH 🔊
-----------------
- Look for a speaker icon on text
- Click it to hear the text read aloud
- Helpful for checking pronunciation

SIGN LANGUAGE PANEL 🤟
----------------------
- On the right side of the screen
- Shows sign language illustrations
- Automatically appears for certain words
- Helps connect English words to signs

LARGE TEXT OPTIONS ♿
--------------------
- Go to Accessibility settings
- Choose larger text size
- High contrast option available

==========================================
TIPS FOR FAMILY LEARNING
==========================================

FOR MUM AND SISTER:

1. SIT TOGETHER
   - Learn alongside Amanda
   - Help type questions if needed
   - Discuss the AI's answers together

2. TAKE TURNS
   - Let Amanda ask her questions first
   - Then ask your own questions
   - Compare what you learned

3. MAKE IT FUN
   - Celebrate correct answers
   - Don't worry about mistakes
   - Learning is a journey!

4. OFFLINE LEARNING
   - Download materials when online
   - Review them later without internet
   - Write questions to ask next time

5. REPEAT LESSONS
   - Watch videos more than once
   - Read transcripts multiple times
   - Ask the AI to explain differently

==========================================
PRACTICE SESSION PLAN
==========================================

Try this 30-minute learning session:

MINUTE 1-5: Choose a subject
- Open the lesson
- Look at what materials are available

MINUTE 5-15: Watch or read
- Watch video OR
- Read through the transcript
- Take notes on paper

MINUTE 15-25: Ask the AI
- Open the AI chat
- Ask at least 3 questions
- Read the answers carefully

MINUTE 25-30: Review
- Talk about what you learned
- Write down one new thing you know
- Give each other a quiz!

==========================================
TROUBLESHOOTING
==========================================

PROBLEM: Can't log in
SOLUTION: Check username and password are correct.
Make sure caps lock is off.

PROBLEM: Page won't load
SOLUTION: Check internet connection. 
Try refreshing the page.

PROBLEM: AI is slow to respond
SOLUTION: Wait a few seconds. 
Good answers take time!

PROBLEM: Can't find a lesson
SOLUTION: Go back to dashboard. 
Check "All Lessons" section.

PROBLEM: Video won't play
SOLUTION: Check internet connection.
Try a different browser.
